## Glitchwire
https://glitchwire.com/news/tsao-lab-reports-the-brain-toggles-between-conscious-and-suppressed-content-15-t/
- Recordings from "thousands of neurons" in two rhesus macaques; switching about every 60 ms.
- Tatiana Engel (Princeton) did the computational analysis.
- Tsao is Chief Scientist for Neuro at the Astera Institute.
- Authors: the findings "expose a limitation of searching for the neural correlates of consciousness within the average activity of specific brain regions".
- Caveats: not peer reviewed; only 2 monkeys; the "timing, not area" framing goes beyond what the preprint shows.

## Discrepancy flags
- Neuron counts: press says "thousands of neurons"; the full text gives 3501 responsive neurons in total, 543 in the gain analysis and 429 in the example raster. These are consistent if "thousands" means the total.
- Dwell time: press says "~60 ms"; the full text gives an autocorrelation mean of 67 ms (median 46 ms) and HMM medians of 64/59 ms. Report it as "約 60 ms（數十毫秒）".
- Paradigm: the existing notes say "binocular rivalry", but the actual paradigm is binocular flash suppression (a rivalry variant).
- Source quality: the existing note Doris_Tsao_大腦意識切換.md is based on a Chinese YouTube retelling (大飛／最佳拍檔), so it is secondary.

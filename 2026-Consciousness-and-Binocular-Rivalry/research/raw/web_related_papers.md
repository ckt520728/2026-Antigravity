# RAW: related literature
Fetched 2026-10-29. Abstracts and press only.

1. **Hesse JK, Tsao DY (2020).** "A new no-report paradigm reveals that face cells encode both consciously perceived and suppressed stimuli." eLife 9:e58360. https://elifesciences.org/articles/58360 (PMC7676863)
   - Recordings in macaque IT face patches; the percept was inferred from eye movements, with no active report.
   - Many IT neurons tracked the conscious percept without any report.
   - Both the perceived and the suppressed stimulus could be decoded on single trials.
   - Reviewers: single cells may multiplex the illusory percept and the physical stimulus.

2. **Shi Y, Bi D, Hesse JK, Lanfranchi FF, Chen S, Tsao DY (2026).** "Rapid concerted switching of the neural code in the inferotemporal cortex." Nature. DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10267-3
   - Dates: Caltech news says published Mar 25 2026; the Caltech repository lists May 7 2026.
   - Preprint: bioRxiv 2023-12-06 (10.1101/2023.12.06.570341).
   - Face cells switch from a general face-detection code to a face-specific code after a concerted population event lasting <20 ms. This happens for faces only, not objects.
   - Caltech news: https://neuroscience.caltech.edu/news/fixed-or-flexible-study-shows-vision-related-neurons-can-rapidly-switch-codes

3. **Kingir, Segawa, Hesse, Kagan, Wilke (2025, bioRxiv).** "Fixation shifts in a novel 'no-report' binocular rivalry paradigm induce saccade-related perceptual switches." https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.02.09.637085v1

4. **Cogitate Consortium (2025).** "Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness." Nature, Apr 30 2025. https://idw-online.de/en/news851431
   - 256 human participants; fMRI, MEG and iEEG; preregistered.
   - No sustained posterior synchronization, which challenges IIT.
   - A general lack of ignition at stimulus offset and limited PFC representation of some conscious dimensions, which challenges GNWT.
   - Neither theory was decisively confirmed or refuted.
